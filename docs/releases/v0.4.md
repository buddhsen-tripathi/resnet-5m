Weights for all completed runs from rounds 10–11: three students under the 5M budget and the two wide teachers used to distill one of them. Every model is trained from scratch on the 50,000 CIFAR-10 training images.

| File | Experiment | Epochs | Parameters | CIFAR-10, no TTA | Flip TTA | Kaggle public |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `R10_t12.pt` | R9 recipe distilled from 12 teachers (R9's ten plus R8_base_300 and R9_combined) | 200 | 4,937,794 | 97.51% | 97.59% | **0.901** |
| `R10_shift.pt` | R10_t12 plus resampling and brightness/contrast augmentation | 200 | 4,937,794 | 97.48% | **97.68%** | 0.894 |
| `R11_wide.pt` | R9 recipe distilled from R9's ten teachers plus the two wide teachers | 200 | 4,937,794 | 97.33% | 97.56% | 0.894 |
| `T_wide_s1.pt` | Wide teacher, seed 1, no distillation | 250 | 36,688,170 | 97.63% | 97.86% | not eligible |
| `T_wide_s2.pt` | Wide teacher, seed 2, no distillation | 250 | 36,688,170 | 97.65% | 97.85% | not eligible |

R9_combined from v0.3 remains our best model (97.70% flip TTA, 0.901 Kaggle public). None of these runs improves on it: two more teachers, shift-targeted augmentation and two much stronger teachers all leave the R9 student within 0.15 points on CIFAR-10. R10_t12 ties R9 on Kaggle and disagrees with it on 82 of the 2,000 hidden images.

The students train a 45,824-parameter contrastive head alongside the classifier, for 4,983,618 total trainable parameters; the head is omitted from these exports. The wide teachers use our pre-activation ResNet at WideResNet-28-10 scale (widths 160-320-640, four blocks per stage, no SE). They exceed the 5M limit, were trained with `--allow-over-budget`, and are distillation teachers only, never submissions. Round 10 ran on a Colab A100 and round 11 on one H200. All runs use one seed.

```bash
gh release download v0.4 -R buddhsen-tripathi/resnet-5m -p '*.pt' -D checkpoints
uv sync
uv run python evaluate.py checkpoints/R10_t12.pt checkpoints/R10_shift.pt checkpoints/R11_wide.pt
uv run python predict.py --checkpoint checkpoints/R10_t12.pt --out submission.csv
```

`evaluate.py` prints each model's flip-TTA accuracy and an ensemble result; the table above reports the individual models. The files contain the student or teacher weights and configuration, work with `evaluate.py`, `predict.py` and `train.py --teacher`, and omit optimizer state, so they cannot be resumed with `--resume`. R10_t12 and R10_shift are the exact files used for their Kaggle submissions. `round10-11.json` records configurations, evaluation results and SHA-256 checksums for all five exports.
